AI · Automation

Project RILEY

A self-hosted AI agent that runs my infrastructure and my day. It lives as its own user on my Linux workstation, keeps its memory in a git-backed document system I designed, and hands itself off between sessions with nobody at the keyboard. It administers the fleet over a Tailscale mesh, pushes schedule and traffic alerts to my phone, slices and starts 3D prints and checks them by camera, watches training runs, and follows scripted troubleshooting paths when something breaks: a killed session is found, diagnosed, and moved home automatically.

more

Front end is a phone app I designed: chat, notes, calendar, file viewer, print jobs, and a lockdown mode that revokes the agent's tools while someone else is holding the phone. Every action the agent takes is logged and every fact it relies on is a file I can read. Built in bash and Python on systemd, with ntfy for push and a small local model trained alongside it.

ADHD and dyslexia

I have both. They haven't stopped me, and technology is why. I believe technology and AI are the keys to leveling the playing field and accommodating every kind of brain. The tools on this page are how I read, remember, and get things done.

Two proven techniques, combined, make reading easier despite dyslexia. The OpenDyslexic font helps with individual words.
